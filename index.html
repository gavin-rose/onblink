    Menu items, in order
    Input

    Double blink uses the SDK's built-in blink detection: blink twice to open the menu, and twice again to pick. Long blink uses OnBlink's own eye tracking from face landmarks: close your eyes for about a second instead. Try long blink if double blinks are missed in your lighting or setup.

    Check-ins

    Your level is learned during the first minute of each session and kept in memory only. It is forgotten when the session stops. Check-ins pause while you are moving and appear at most once every 10 minutes.

    Read options aloud

    Options are only a guide for the person using OnBlink, so keep them quiet, or send them to headphones or a single earbud. The phrase you pick is always spoken through the Mac's main output so people nearby can hear it.

    Session

    Leave this on Never for everyday use so OnBlink keeps listening. A time limit is handy for testing, demos, or keeping an eye on usage of a free Presage key.

    Before you start

    OnBlink uses your camera to follow your eyes and estimate your heart rate. Here is exactly what happens:

    • Camera: on only after you press Start, and stays on until you press Stop, or until the optional time limit in Settings.
    • Processing: video frames are analyzed by the SmartSpectra SDK from Presage Technologies. Presage's documentation says vitals are computed on this device. The SDK also makes authenticated requests to Presage's servers, for example to check the API key and usage.
    • Presage telemetry: turned off in OnBlink. Presage says that when it is off, the SDK sends no telemetry. Even when on, Presage says it never includes video, measured values, or identifiers.
    • What OnBlink saves: only your menu items and settings, on this computer. No video, eye data, or heart rate is ever saved or sent anywhere by OnBlink.
    • Check-ins (optional): if you turn them on, OnBlink compares your heart rate to your own level from earlier in the same session and may offer to start a conversation. Nothing is said or sent unless you choose it.

    OnBlink is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or monitor any condition. Do not rely on it in an emergency.
